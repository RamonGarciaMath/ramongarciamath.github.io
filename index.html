About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Iowa State University, working with Shira Zerbib and Bernard Lidický. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign, advised by József Balogh. Previously, I earned my bachelor's degree in Mathematics from the Universidad de Guanajuato, where my advisor was Octavio Arizmendi.
